# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Frontend engineers, product teams, and coding agents starting new CORELIA-style
Next.js product applications. They need a clear starting point, predictable
ownership boundaries, and a reliable way to verify changes before handoff.

## Product Purpose

CORELIA Next Boilerplate provides a feature-first foundation for authenticated
SaaS-style frontend apps with REST backends, Docker/Nginx deployment, GitLab CI,
and agent-friendly development rules. Success means teams can add product areas
quickly without inventing architecture or overbuilding shared abstractions.

## Brand Personality

Calm, practical, opinionated.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS marketing gloss, oversized decorative hero sections, heavy
platform abstractions, and architecture that solves hypothetical future needs
before a feature actually needs them.

## Design Principles

- Show the workflow, not a sales pitch.
- Make the safest path the obvious path.
- Prefer small contracts over large frameworks.
- Let examples teach agents what to copy.
- Keep verification visible and repeatable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA basics: semantic regions, keyboard access, visible focus,
readable contrast, reduced motion support, and labels for interactive controls.
